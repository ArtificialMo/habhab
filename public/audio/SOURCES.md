# Carboy audio sources

These locally bundled OGG files are recorded sound effects, not MIDI or generated
tones. They are loaded by `src/audio/audio.ts` after the first user gesture.

- `coin-drop.ogg` — “Dropmetalthing.ogg”, recorded by Secretlondon. The recording is
  described as a metal object, possibly a coin, dropped onto a surface and spinning.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dropmetalthing.ogg
  License: CC BY-SA 3.0 (the file page also lists GFDL as an available license).
- `metal-impact.ogg` — “Metal Clanging Noises.ogg”, recorded by Camshaft64. The
  recording captures pieces of aluminum impacting asphalt. It supplies the physical
  metal layer for car collisions.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Metal_Clanging_Noises.ogg
  License: CC BY-SA 4.0.

- engine-loop.ogg - "MAC3.OGG", a recorded V8 engine clip by Mustangworld. It is
  layered quietly under the responsive synthetic engine bed.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:MAC3.OGG
  License: CC BY-SA 3.0.

The files are redistributed unchanged under their source licenses. Attribution is
kept here with the shipped assets so the production site carries the credit with the
runtime files.
